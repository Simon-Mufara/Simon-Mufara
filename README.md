## About Me

I am a bioinformatics researcher and software developer currently completing an **MSc in Computational Health Informatics** at the University of Cape Town (Division of Computational Biology, CBIO), funded by a **DSI-NRF Freestanding Scholarship**. My MSc thesis centres on transformer-based multi-omics integration for drug response prediction in African breast cancer cohorts.


My work spans:

- transformer and deep learning models for multi-omics drug response prediction
- reproducible genomic variant calling and sequencing pipelines
- clinical bioinformatics web applications and APIs
- healthcare data analytics and AI-assisted reporting
- African-contextualised precision medicine and genomics infrastructure

I build systems that are not just interesting in theory but **usable, reproducible, and deployment-ready** — from HPC pipelines to interactive clinical tools.
